/ 02 — Our Story

Roasted on Tuesdays. In your cup by Friday.

Every batch is roasted to order — never warehoused, never freezer-burned. We buy direct from 14 origin partners across Ethiopia, Colombia, Guatemala, and Kenya, and we pay 2–3× commodity price.

The name comes from the line we roast on: a 1978 Probat that our head roaster, Amara, rebuilt by hand. It runs 12kg batches. Not 500kg. Small enough to taste the difference.
