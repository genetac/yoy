   591→| --- | --- | --- |

   593→| 10-slide report | 25 min with discussion | ~120s per slide; misses slide gets 240s |

   597→

   599→  {{client_company}}." Don't introduce yourself for two minutes — your
   600→  name is on the strip; the agenda is more valuable than your bio.
   601→- **L02 cover-agenda:** Beat: the three things. Say them as verbs.
   602→  Bridge: "Let's start with the one you already feel."

   604→  a palate reset — use the silence, don't fill it.

   606→  evidence line. Never paraphrase your own thesis slide — it dilutes

   609→  for three seconds. The pause is the delivery.

   630→  silently for five seconds, then add the one thing that's not written.
